# Questions and answers

24 questions a careful reader of this book can answer, a paddler's, an angler's or a town planner's, from the plainest (one river, one day) to the hardest (which year was wettest, and how floods have changed), each with its answer and the page to look in. Read a question, open the page, and find the answer yourself; or put the questions to a reader or an agent with this page hidden, and mark what comes back. Where the book cannot answer, the right answer is to say so. The answers are worked out afresh from USGS's own files at every release, not copied from the pages: where the two disagree, one is wrong.
